PERLIN ATELIER 1.1.1 — MAC INSTALLER

1. Quit any older copy of Perlin Atelier.
2. Double-click Perlin-Atelier-1.1.1-macOS-Universal-Installer.pkg.
3. Choose Continue, then Install. Enter your Mac administrator password if asked.
4. Open Finder > Applications > Perlin Atelier.

The installer places the app at /Applications/Perlin Atelier.app.
It works on Apple silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 12 or later.
No Python, Node.js, browser, internet connection or other downloads are needed.
The app runs in its own window. SVG and PNG export use the Mac Save dialogue.

Choose Letter garden, enter your text and explore the five stages. Select
Decorate for the finished composition. Import SVGs into your plotter software
at actual size. The app generates artwork and does not control your plotter.

The app is ad-hoc signed. The installer is unsigned and is not notarised by
Apple. macOS may block the initial opening. If you trust this specific download,
follow Apple's per-app approval instructions:
https://support.apple.com/en-gb/102445
Do not disable Gatekeeper or other system-wide protections.

The installer contains only the app and has no installation scripts. Your
exported drawings are not changed. No restart is needed. Uninstall by moving
Perlin Atelier from Applications to the Bin.

Source and build instructions: https://github.com/GWB2025/perlin-atelier
